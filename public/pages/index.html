04 / Scoring protocol

How FitScore works

FitScore is an editorial tool for hypertrophy-oriented exercise selection—not a scientific measurement or a substitute for coaching. Each score starts with the rounded weighted baseline shown here, then may receive an exercise-specific editorial adjustment of up to four points when the five factors miss a meaningful trade-off.

Score build: Rounded 30/20/20/20/10 weighted baseline + editorial adjustment (±4 maximum). Every exercise detail shows both numbers.

05 / Use the list well

Use the rankings in your training

Rankings help you shortlist. Your logbook decides. Keep an exercise if it feels stable, reaches the target, tolerates progressive loading, and does not create unwanted joint pain.

01

Start repeatable. Pick 1–2 exercises per target that you can execute consistently.

02

Train with intent. Most working sets should finish with roughly 1–3 good reps left.

03

Progress one variable. Add a rep, small load, or cleaner range before adding novelty.
