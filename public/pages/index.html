02 / Scoring protocol

NO MAGIC.
JUST TRADE-OFFS.

FitScore is an editorial tool for hypertrophy-oriented exercise selection—not a scientific measurement or a substitute for coaching. Each score starts with the rounded weighted baseline shown here, then may receive an exercise-specific editorial adjustment of up to four points when the five factors miss a meaningful trade-off.

03 / Use the list well

THE BEST MOVE IS THE ONE YOU CAN PROGRESS.

Rankings help you shortlist. Your logbook decides. Keep a movement if it feels stable, reaches the target, tolerates progressive loading, and does not create unwanted joint pain.

01

Start repeatable. Pick 1–2 movements per target that you can execute consistently.

02

Train with intent. Most working sets should finish with roughly 1–3 good reps left.

03

Progress one variable. Add a rep, small load, or cleaner range before adding novelty.

05 / About the founder

BUILT IN THE UAE.
MADE TO MAKE TRAINING CLEARER.

STRATA was founded by Saeed Abdalla Alketbi at 22, while he was a third-year chemical engineering student at United Arab Emirates University (UAEU). Born and raised in the UAE and based in Al Ain, Saeed created STRATA to make exercise selection clearer, more practical, and easier to understand.

His engineering studies shape the way STRATA works: break complicated decisions into transparent factors, explain the reasoning, and give every athlete a useful starting point for better training.
