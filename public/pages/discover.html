What do these scores mean?
FitScore
STRATA’s fixed exercise score. It does not change based on your profile.
Match for you
How well the exercise fits your goals, equipment, experience, and saved limitations.
Community rating
The average overall rating submitted by STRATA members, on a five-point scale.

FitScore is an editorial rating out of 100. Equipment and limitations determine eligibility; Match for you ranks eligible exercises using your saved preferences.
