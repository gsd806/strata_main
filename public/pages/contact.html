WHAT TO INCLUDE

For account support, include the email address used for STRATA. If a verification, password-reset, or deletion email has not arrived, wait briefly and check the spam or junk folder before requesting another. For a payment or refund request, include the account email and Paddle order or transaction reference if one was provided.

Keep secrets private.
Never email or forward a verification code, password-reset link, account-deletion link, password, full card number, security code, Turso token, Resend key, email-verification secret, Paddle API key or webhook secret, or Render credentials. STRATA support does not need them.

For urgent medical concerns, contact a qualified medical professional or local emergency service. STRATA provides general fitness education, not medical care.
