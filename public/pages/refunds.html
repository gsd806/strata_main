14-DAY
REFUNDS.

Last updated 4 September 2026

A paid STRATA Discovery purchase may be refunded within 14 calendar days after the purchase date. You do not need to provide a reason.

TermsPrivacyRefunds

Refund window: Send your request within 14 calendar days after the date of your paid Discovery purchase to receive a full refund of the purchase price.

1. How to request a refund

Email stratafitness.official@gmail.com from the email connected to your STRATA account. Include your Paddle order or transaction reference if one was provided. Do not send your password or full payment-card details.

2. How refunds work

A qualifying request made within the 14-day window is returned through Paddle to the original payment method. Paddle and your bank or card provider determine when the refunded amount appears. Discovery access for the refunded account ends when the refund is processed.

3. After 14 days

Change-of-mind refunds are not normally offered after the 14-day period. Please still contact us about a duplicate charge, non-delivery of paid access, or a material technical problem so it can be investigated. Nothing in this policy limits refund, chargeback, or consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.

4. Fully discounted purchases

If a valid promotion reduces the purchase total to $0, there is no paid amount to refund. Access granted by a promotion may be subject to its eligibility terms.

5. Deleting an account

Deleting a STRATA account is not a refund and permanently removes its local Discovery access and purchase mapping. If you may qualify for a refund, contact us from the registered email and include the Paddle transaction reference before deleting the account.

6. Questions

Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, or contact stratafitness.official@gmail.com.
