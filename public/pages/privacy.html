PRIVACY
POLICY.

Last updated 5 September 2026

This policy explains the personal and technical information STRATA handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

TermsPrivacyRefunds

STRATA uses account information to provide sign-in, cross-device workout logging, history, weekly and monthly planning, optional community weekly-plan publishing, ratings, and personalized Strata+. It does not sell or rent personal data and does not currently use behavioral-advertising or analytics cookies.

1. Who handles your data

STRATA Fitness operates the STRATA website and account service. Privacy questions and data requests can be sent to stratafitness.official@gmail.com.

2. Account and fitness-profile data

During a new signup, STRATA temporarily stores the submitted name and email address, a salted scrypt password hash, challenge timestamps and counters, and an HMAC digest of the six-digit verification code. The plaintext password and verification code are not stored in the database. No user account or normal signed-in session is created until the code is accepted.

After verification, STRATA stores your name, email address, account creation time, and salted password hash. It also stores hashed session identifiers, session expiry information, weekly plans, training goals, experience level, available equipment, exercise preferences, optional movement constraints, exercise ratings, and relevant update timestamps. For a saved Strata+ monthly plan, STRATA stores its title, start date, exercises-per-target choice, weekday muscle-target and rest schedule, imported or copied weekly source choices, generated exercise entries and prescriptions, and update time. Individual ratings are connected to your account in storage but are shown to other users only as exercise-level aggregates. Your Strata+ profile and monthly plan are not publicly displayed.

When you use signed-in workout logging, STRATA stores session dates, titles, exercise identifiers, planned prescriptions, actual repetitions, recorded loads and units, timed sets, completion state, rest-timer deadlines, elapsed time, and update revisions. Your private history and progress charts are calculated from completed logged sets. Workouts are scoped to your account and are removed with account deletion.

If you choose to publish your week, STRATA stores one listing for your account containing its title, optional description, validated structured weekly plan, an internal owner identifier, publication state, your STRATA display name, and creation and update timestamps. Strata+ members can see the title, description, plan, display name, and timestamps. Your email address and internal user identifier are not included in the listing returned to them. Applying somebody else's plan creates a separate copy in your private weekly Plan and does not reveal your identity to its author. Do not put private personal information in a community-plan title or description.

For password reset and account deletion, STRATA temporarily stores a hash of a random one-time link, its purpose, expiry and delivery state, and security-email rate-limit records. The raw link is present in the email delivered by Resend and is processed transiently by your browser and STRATA’s server, but STRATA’s database stores only its hash. A new plaintext password is processed only to create a new per-user salted hash and is not stored. A password reset replaces the existing salted hash and signs out every device.

If you contact support, STRATA stores the name, email, category, subject, message, optional order reference, status, internal support note, response time, and related timestamps. A signed-in request is attached to the account’s stored name, email, and user identifier. Never include a password, verification code, recovery or deletion link, full card number, or API key.

3. Trial and payment information

When a signed-in account starts the one-time Strata+ trial, STRATA stores the account identifier and trial start and expiry times so access can follow the account across devices and the trial cannot be restarted.

Paddle handles checkout and payment information under its own privacy notice. STRATA receives transaction, customer, product, entitlement, purchase-status, discount, and refund information needed to provide Strata+ access and support. STRATA does not receive or store full payment-card or bank-account details.

4. Technical information

The service temporarily uses an IP address for signup, verification, login, resend, password-reset, account-deletion, support-form, and rating rate limits. This includes limits on requesting, checking, and completing one-time account-action links. For support-form protection, STRATA stores one-way hashes of the network address and submitted email with a timestamp; the application database does not store the raw network address in that quota record. Render and other infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request times, requested URLs, browser or device headers, and error information to deliver, diagnose, and protect the service.

Authorized administration records the administrator account, affected account identifier where applicable, action, reason, result, and time. The administrator can view account identity and status, session counts, plan and rating totals, limited Paddle transaction status, pending deletion status, and support requests. The dashboard never displays password hashes, session or action tokens, verification-code digests, payment-card data, database credentials, or provider API secrets.

5. Why information is used

  • To verify new signup email addresses and send essential verification, recovery, and deletion-confirmation messages
  • To create, authenticate, recover, and delete accounts
  • To sync profiles, workouts, and weekly and monthly plans across devices
  • To publish weekly plans at an account holder's request and let Strata+ members browse and apply them
  • To calculate personalized Strata+ results and generate user-directed monthly plans
  • To calculate aggregated community ratings
  • To provide paid access, receipts, support, and refunds
  • To prevent misuse, enforce request limits, and maintain service security
  • To meet applicable legal obligations

Depending on the law that applies, STRATA relies on performing its agreement with you, taking steps you request before entering that agreement, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where consent is required. You may contact us with a question about the basis used for a particular activity.

6. Providers and external services

STRATA uses Render for application hosting, Turso for database hosting, Resend for essential signup-verification, password-recovery, and deletion-confirmation email delivery, and Paddle as merchant of record for checkout and payments. To deliver an account message, Resend receives the recipient and sender addresses, subject and message content, and related delivery metadata. Resend states that email content and metadata, delivery events, logs, and metrics are retained for 30 days across its standard plans; see Resend’s Privacy Policy and current retention documentation.

Paddle’s checkout software loads on the Pricing page and may receive normal technical request information needed to provide checkout. The public site loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash, which may receive normal request information such as your IP address and browser headers. YouTube, research, and other external sites receive information when you follow their links. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

7. Cookies, imports, and device storage

Guest workout logs, week templates, setup choices, and recovery drafts use browser local storage. Account drafts and setup choices are separated by account identifier; this separation is not encryption. Anyone with access to this browser profile or its storage may access device copies. Signing out does not erase recovery drafts. Remove device drafts through the workout recovery controls, or clear site data in your browser when you no longer need it. Clearing site data loses guest plans, guest history, local templates, and unsynced drafts; it does not delete saved account data. Setup choices are remembered only in this browser, while an explicitly saved account week syncs across devices.

The login-free planner stores its plan in your browser's local storage on that device. STRATA's server does not receive that guest plan during ordinary guest use. If you explicitly export that plan, sign in to Strata+, locally import the STRATA JSON file, and save or generate a monthly plan from it, the imported choices become part of the private monthly plan stored with your account.

The file picker reads an imported STRATA weekly-plan JSON file locally in your browser; STRATA does not upload or retain the original file as a separate attachment. Only validated choices you explicitly save to your signed-in weekly or monthly plan are sent to the STRATA server. Likewise, publishing a saved week sends validated structured plan choices to STRATA rather than uploading a binary file or attachment.

During signup, STRATA uses a necessary, HttpOnly pre-authentication cookie to associate the browser with its pending verification challenge. It ends with the challenge and lasts no longer than 30 minutes. After verification or login, STRATA uses a separate necessary account-session cookie. The account-session cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, secure in production, and can remain valid for up to seven days. Neither cookie is used for advertising or analytics.

The installed PWA caches public pages and interface files on your device. Its service worker does not cache account APIs, authentication requests, private Strata+ pages, verification responses, password-reset or account-deletion pages, one-time links, community-plan responses, or saved account plan data. Guest plan data stays in browser local storage and is not placed in the service worker cache.

8. Sharing, printing, and selling

STRATA does not sell or rent personal data. Information may be shared with the providers listed above, when legally required, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce these Terms, and operate the service. STRATA does not currently use third-party behavioral advertising.

Publishing is optional. When you confirm publication, the structured weekly plan and your STRATA display name become visible inside the Strata+ community-plan browser until you unpublish the listing or delete your account. Your email address remains private. Applying a community plan explicitly replaces your current saved week; it does not edit the author's listing.

Choosing a monthly-plan share control deliberately passes the generated plan text or file to your operating system's share sheet, a selected app, or your device clipboard. Choosing Download PDF opens the browser or operating system print interface, where you decide whether and where to save or send the output. Those actions can move plan information outside STRATA and make it subject to the selected app, clipboard, printer, storage location, or recipient's practices; STRATA does not share a plan through those controls until you choose the action.

9. Retention

A verification code expires after 10 minutes and its pending signup challenge ends after 30 minutes. Password-reset and deletion links expire after 30 minutes. Used, expired, or abandoned challenges are invalidated and cleaned up. One-way support-form quota records are cleaned up after 24 hours. Active account data, private weekly and monthly plans, published weekly-plan copies, preferences, and ratings are retained while the account remains in use. A plan owner can unpublish their shared copy without deleting the private weekly plan. Support requests and administrator security logs are retained while reasonably needed to answer requests, document account actions, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Completing self-service deletion permanently removes the STRATA user record, sessions, private weekly plan, published weekly-plan copies, preferences, ratings, local Strata+ entitlement and purchase mapping, and the linked monthly-plan row through the database's account-deletion cascade. A community plan already applied by somebody else is an independent copy in that person's private Plan and is not removed when the original listing or author account is deleted. A separately submitted support request may retain the sender’s name, email, message, replies, and reference after account deletion, while an administrator security log may retain the deleted account’s internal identifier and action history. Contact STRATA to request review or deletion of a support record where applicable. Global webhook event identifiers that do not identify the account may remain for security and duplicate-delivery prevention. Paddle, Resend, Render, and other providers may retain transaction, delivery, fraud-prevention, tax, legal, or technical records under their own policies and legal obligations. Account deletion does not delete Paddle’s merchant-of-record records or create a refund.

10. Your choices and rights

You decide whether to publish a weekly plan and can unpublish your own shared listing from Plan. A signed-out person can request a password-reset link from the Forgot Password page; STRATA gives the same response whether or not the supplied address has an account. A signed-in user can request a password-reset email or a separate account-deletion confirmation email from the Account page, and STRATA sends it only to the registered address. Deletion only occurs after opening the one-time link and typing the required confirmation; merely opening the email never deletes data. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of account data by emailing stratafitness.official@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable proof that you control the account. Additional privacy rights may apply under the laws where you live.

11. Security

STRATA uses salted password hashing, hashed session and one-time-link tokens, HMAC-only verification-code storage, short expiry periods, attempt and resend limits, secure production cookies, parameterized database queries, same-origin write checks, and rate limiting. Verification codes permit at most five incorrect attempts, and resend requests have a 60-second cooldown. Password resets rotate the account’s credential version and revoke all sessions so a login racing with an old password cannot survive.

The private administrator area is limited to one verified, permanently bound account selected by server configuration. It requires a normal signed-in session plus a fresh password confirmation that expires after 30 minutes; confirming the password rotates both the session and CSRF credentials. Sensitive account changes require CSRF and same-origin checks, typed confirmation, a reason, and an audit record. The administrator can send self-service links only to the registered address, revoke sessions, pause or restore an account, and manage support requests; the administrator cannot view passwords or directly complete account deletion. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, never share a verification code or recovery link, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

12. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their contractual, organizational, and legal safeguards apply to that processing.

13. Children

STRATA is not directed to children who cannot legally consent to account-data processing where they live. A parent or guardian should supervise use by minors and make any purchase.
