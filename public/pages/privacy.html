PRIVACY
POLICY.

Last updated 3 September 2026

This policy explains the personal and technical information STRATA handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

TermsPrivacyRefunds

STRATA uses account information to provide sign-in, cross-device syncing, planning, ratings, and personalized Discovery. It does not sell or rent personal data and does not currently use behavioral-advertising or analytics cookies.

1. Who handles your data

STRATA Fitness operates the STRATA website and account service. Privacy questions and data requests can be sent to stratafitness.official@gmail.com.

2. Account and fitness-profile data

During a new signup, STRATA temporarily stores the submitted name and email address, a salted scrypt password hash, challenge timestamps and counters, and an HMAC digest of the six-digit verification code. The plaintext password and verification code are not stored. No user account or normal signed-in session is created until the code is accepted.

After verification, STRATA stores your name, email address, account creation time, and salted password hash. It also stores hashed session identifiers, session expiry information, weekly plans, training goals, experience level, available equipment, exercise preferences, optional movement constraints, exercise ratings, and relevant update timestamps. Individual ratings are connected to your account in storage but are shown to other users only as exercise-level aggregates. Your Discovery profile is not publicly displayed.

3. Payment information

Paddle handles checkout and payment information under its own privacy notice. STRATA receives transaction, customer, product, entitlement, purchase-status, discount, and refund information needed to provide Discovery access and support. STRATA does not receive or store full payment-card or bank-account details.

4. Technical information

The service temporarily uses an IP address for signup, verification, login, resend, and rating rate limits. Render and other infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, request times, requested URLs, browser or device headers, and error information to deliver, diagnose, and protect the service.

5. Why information is used

  • To verify new signup email addresses and send essential verification messages
  • To create and authenticate accounts
  • To sync profiles and weekly plans across devices
  • To calculate personalized Discovery results
  • To calculate aggregated community ratings
  • To provide paid access, receipts, support, and refunds
  • To prevent misuse, enforce request limits, and maintain service security
  • To meet applicable legal obligations

Depending on the law that applies, STRATA relies on performing its agreement with you, taking steps you request before entering that agreement, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the service, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where consent is required. You may contact us with a question about the basis used for a particular activity.

6. Providers and external services

STRATA uses Render for application hosting, Turso for database hosting, Resend for essential signup-verification email delivery, and Paddle as merchant of record for checkout and payments. To deliver a verification message, Resend receives the recipient and sender addresses, subject and message content, and related delivery metadata. Resend states that email content and metadata, delivery events, logs, and metrics are retained for 30 days across its standard plans; see Resend’s Privacy Policy and current retention documentation.

Paddle’s checkout software loads on the Pricing page and may receive normal technical request information needed to provide checkout. The public site loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash, which may receive normal request information such as your IP address and browser headers. YouTube, research, and other external sites receive information when you follow their links. Those services apply their own privacy policies.

7. Cookies and device storage

During signup, STRATA uses a necessary, HttpOnly pre-authentication cookie to associate the browser with its pending verification challenge. It ends with the challenge and lasts no longer than 30 minutes. After verification or login, STRATA uses a separate necessary account-session cookie. The account-session cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict, secure in production, and can remain valid for up to seven days. Neither cookie is used for advertising or analytics.

The installed PWA caches public pages and interface files on your device. Its service worker does not cache account APIs, authentication requests, private Discovery pages, verification responses, or saved plan data.

8. Sharing and selling

STRATA does not sell or rent personal data. Information may be shared with the providers listed above, when legally required, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, enforce these Terms, and operate the service. STRATA does not currently use third-party behavioral advertising.

9. Retention

A verification code expires after 10 minutes and its pending signup challenge ends after 30 minutes. Used, expired, or abandoned challenges are invalidated and cleaned up; they never become user accounts unless verification succeeds. Active account data, plans, preferences, and ratings are retained while the account remains in use or until deletion is requested, subject to legal, security, and operational retention needs. Account sessions expire after seven days. Resend currently retains verification-email content and related delivery records for 30 days. Hosting logs and payment records are retained under the relevant provider’s policies and legal obligations.

10. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by emailing stratafitness.official@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable proof that you control the account. Additional privacy rights may apply under the laws where you live.

11. Security

STRATA uses salted password hashing, hashed session tokens, HMAC-only verification-code storage, short expiry periods, attempt and resend limits, secure production cookies, parameterized database queries, same-origin write checks, and rate limiting. Verification codes permit at most five incorrect attempts, and resend requests have a 60-second cooldown. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Use a unique password, never share a verification code, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

12. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their contractual, organizational, and legal safeguards apply to that processing.

13. Children

STRATA is not directed to children who cannot legally consent to account-data processing where they live. A parent or guardian should supervise use by minors and make any purchase.

14. Changes and contact

Updates will be posted here with a revised date. Contact stratafitness.official@gmail.com with questions or requests. See the Terms of Service and Refund Policy for related information.
